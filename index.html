Online Scam Library

Understand different scams and their warning signs.

📧

Phishing Scams

Fake emails or messages designed to steal personal information.

Warning signs:
• Fake bank or company messages
• Suspicious links
• Requests for passwords or OTPs

Protection: Visit official websites directly instead of clicking unexpected links.
🛒

Online Shopping Scams

Fake sellers or stores that take payment without delivering products.

Warning signs:
• Prices that seem too good to be true
• No verified reviews
• Sellers demanding direct payment

Protection: Check seller ratings, reviews, and official payment options.
💰

Investment Scams

Fraudulent offers promising unrealistic profits or guaranteed returns.

Warning signs:
• Guaranteed high profits
• Pressure to invest immediately
• Referral-based earning schemes

Protection: Verify the company and its authorization before investing.
📱

OTP Scams

Scammers trick people into sharing one-time passwords or verification codes.

Warning signs:
• Someone asks for your OTP
• Unexpected verification requests
• Messages claiming your account will be blocked

Protection: Never share OTPs, PINs, or verification codes.
❤️

Romance Scams

Fake online relationships used to manipulate victims into sending money.

Warning signs:
• Requests for money from someone you barely know
• Refusal to meet or video call
• Emotional pressure and urgent stories

Protection: Do not send money or private information to unverified online contacts.
🎁

Fake Giveaway Scams

Fake prizes used to collect personal information or payment.

Warning signs:
• Unexpected prize notifications
• Requests for processing fees
• Suspicious accounts pretending to be brands

Protection: Verify giveaways through the official organization's account.
